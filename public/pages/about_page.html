About RECAP

An AI-powered capstone metadata repository built to make past student research discoverable, citable, and reusable — for everyone at CTU Daanbantayan.

Start Searching Meet the Team
The Problem
Research was hard to find
Before RECAP, capstone projects at CTU Daanbantayan were stored in physical binders or scattered digital folders with no way to search, filter, or verify originality without visiting the library in person.
No central archive
Projects stored physically with no digital index or organization.
Poor discoverability
No way to search by topic, year, or keyword across all projects.
Duplicate research
Students unknowingly proposed topics that had already been studied.
Library hours only
Access limited to physical visits during operating hours.
What RECAP Offers
Built around four core features
Intelligent Search
Filter by keyword, year, program, or author. AI-powered semantic search for natural language queries.
AI Chatbot
Ask questions in natural language. The chatbot guides you through the system 24/7.
Metadata Repository
Structured records with title, abstract, authors, adviser, year, and suggested future research.
Role-Based Access
Students browse, advisers validate titles, and librarians manage records — all with proper permissions.
How It Works
Three simple steps
01
Search
Type a keyword or ask the AI a question to find relevant capstone projects.
02
Discover
Browse metadata cards, view abstracts, methodology, and suggested future research.
03
Cite
Use the built-in citation tool to properly credit the original researchers.
