The Academic Challenge
Why Traditional Research Archives Fall Short
Before RE-CAPS, undergraduate researchers and faculty advisers at CTU Daanbantayan faced systemic hurdles when trying to discover, evaluate, and cite past capstone manuscripts.
Physical Binder Silos
Decades of capstones locked in shelf binders with no digital index, accessible only during library hours.
Keyword Search Blindness
Traditional exact keyword searches miss relevant studies that use conceptual synonyms, acronyms, or differing academic phrasing.
Unintentional Duplication
Students frequently proposed capstone ideas that had already been defended in prior semesters due to lack of cross-year visibility.
Citation Overhead
Manual bibliography formatting in APA, IEEE, or MLA caused citation inaccuracies and wasted critical writing time.
Architecture Breakdown
How the RE-CAPS RAG Pipeline Works
Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) grounds language models in verified university thesis data, eliminating AI hallucinations and providing source citations.
Stage 01
Vector Ingestion
Each thesis title, abstract, methodology, and key findings are transformed into 1024-dimensional dense vectors using the multilingual-e5-large model.
multilingual-e5-large
Stage 02
Pinecone Similarity
When a student searches, their natural language query is vectorized in real time and matched against the Pinecone index using cosine similarity (80%+ threshold).
Pinecone Vector DB
Stage 03
Context Grounding
The top relevant manuscripts, author credits, advisers, and key discoveries are injected into the AI context window as authoritative ground truth.
System Prompt Grounding
Stage 04
Cascading Synthesis
A 4-tier cascading engine (Mistral → Groq → Gemini → OpenRouter) streams word-by-word academic responses with real citations via Server-Sent Events.
SSE Streaming API

Built for Real Academic Discovery
RE-CAPS is not just a digital catalog — it is a complete, production-grade research platform built with modern cloud and AI infrastructure.
Dual Search Engine
Instant Boolean filtering by year, program, and adviser combined with Pinecone vector semantic search for contextual queries.
4-Tier Cascading AI
