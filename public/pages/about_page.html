01 / INSTITUTIONAL CONTEXT

Solving Manual Shelf Bottlenecks Without Compromising Manuscript Security

Baseline evaluation across 363 campus stakeholders confirmed severe manual search friction (Mean 4.32 / 5.00). RE-CAPS indexes structured metadata and physical call numbers—never exposed PDFs—so researchers find relevant studies in seconds while honoring institutional copyright.

BASELINE

Manual Binder & Logbook Process

  • Physical Shelf Silos Hardbound capstones searchable only in person during campus library hours, averaging 20–30 minutes per manual stack search.
  • Exact-Keyword Blindness Spreadsheets and paper ledgers miss conceptually related studies phrased with different methodologies or terminology.
  • Unintentional Topic Duplication Capstone 1 teams lacked cross-year, cross-program visibility during title defense proposals.
  • Manual Citation Friction Hand-formatting APA 7th, MLA 9th, and IEEE entries led to inconsistent bibliographies and referencing errors.
RE-CAPS

AI-Integrated Metadata & Shelf Locator

  • 24/7 Structured Metadata + Call Numbers Instant remote discovery of titles, abstracts, advisers, authors, and exact physical rack/row shelf coordinates.
  • 1024-D Semantic Vector Retrieval Natural-language queries retrieve conceptually matched studies via Pinecone cosine similarity across all degree programs.
  • Pre-Defense Novelty Verification Faculty advisers and students inspect prior work across semesters before approving Capstone 1 titles.
  • R.A. 10173 Copy Guard + 1-Click Citations Titles and abstracts are copy-protected against plagiarism while authors remain whitelisted and citations export cleanly.

Institutional Security, Privacy & Quota Architecture

Click any safeguard to inspect implementation details

Capstone titles and abstracts are strictly protected against copying, text selection, dragging, right-clicking, and mobile touch-holding across all portals (text-protection.js), while research authors remain 100% whitelisted and copyable for legitimate academic attribution. Full-text PDFs are never exposed publicly.

Prevents API exhaustion on free-tier LLM keys with server-enforced daily query allocations (25/day Students, 50/day Faculty, 150/day Librarians & Admins) resetting at 12:00 AM PHT, live X-RateLimit-* & X-Model-Health headers, automatic 429 cooldown routing, and zero Firestore write overhead.

Prevents concurrent editing collisions when multiple librarians catalog physical shelf coordinates (callNumber, accessionNumber, rack/row notes) using atomic Firestore leases with 45-second TTL heartbeat renewal.

Account registration performs client-side OCR verification of CTU Student/Faculty ID numbers with unique ID locking and trajectory-jitter velocity analysis to block automated bot sign-ups.

Administrator and Librarian accounts enforce server-verified personal Gmail 2FA via Resend OTP before unlocking privileged catalog writes, user governance sanctions, or batch Pinecone synchronization.
