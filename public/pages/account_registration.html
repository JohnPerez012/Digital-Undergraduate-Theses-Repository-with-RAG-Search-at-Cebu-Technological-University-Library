RE-CAPS Platform Terms of Service & System Transparency

Academic Year: 2026–2027 • Platform Version: 2.5

Governing Campus: Cebu Technological University - Daanbantayan Campus

Welcome to RE-CAPS (Research Capstone Archiving and Retrieval System). This document outlines our official terms of service, acceptable use policies, and a 100% transparent disclosure of how our architecture, databases, AI models, and client-side privacy safeguards handle your academic data.
Section 1 • Institutional Scope

Institutional Purpose & Zero-Monetization

RE-CAPS is an official, non-profit academic research repository and semantic discovery platform operated exclusively for Cebu Technological University - Daanbantayan Campus.

1.1 Supported Academic Programs: The catalog houses verified capstone and research manuscripts across accredited programs:

  • BSIT: Bachelor of Science in Information Technology
  • BSFi: Bachelor of Science in Fisheries
  • BSHM: Bachelor of Science in Hospitality Management
  • BEEd: Bachelor of Elementary Education
  • BSEd: Bachelor of Secondary Education
  • BIT: Bachelor of Industrial Technology
Zero-Monetization & Free Academic Access: RE-CAPS is 100% free for all authorized students, faculty, and university personnel. We do not charge subscription fees, sell user data, display commercial advertisements, or monetize institutional research.
Section 2 • Registration & Verification

Identity Registration & Client-Side OCR

To maintain academic integrity and prevent unauthorized access, user registration follows a structured verification protocol:

  • User-Provided Legal Identity: In Step 1, you enter your legal First Name, Middle Name, and Last Name.
  • In-Browser Optical Character Recognition (Tesseract.js): In Step 2, your physical CTU ID card is parsed inside your web browser using HTML5 Canvas. The AI optical scanner identifies institutional markers, academic courses, and student/employee numbers in local memory.
  • No Cloud ID Photo Harvesting: Your captured ID photo is used strictly in-browser for visual credential confirmation and client-side OCR. Raw photographs of your ID are not permanently harvested or sold to third-party databases.
  • Federated Google Authentication: Step 3 utilizes Google Sign-In (Firebase Authentication) for verified institutional/personal email binding, eliminating the need to transmit external Google passwords to our platform.
  • Credentials & Recovery Security: Passwords are encrypted using high-security cryptographic hashing (bcrypt/scrypt). Account recovery questions (preset or custom-written) and answers are stored securely for account recovery.
Section 3 • Data Transparency

Dual-Storage Transparency: Cloud vs. Local Device

RE-CAPS implements a strict Dual-Database Architecture designed to maximize user privacy while ensuring database sustainability on institutional cloud infrastructure:

Data Category Storage Location System Purpose
Account Profiles & Roles
Name, email, role, CTU ID number, college, program		 Cloud Firestore Account authentication, role-based access control (Student, Faculty, Librarian, Admin).

Zero-Cloud Activity Tracking Guarantee: Unlike commercial platforms that monitor and profile your clicks on remote servers, RE-CAPS stores all your search logs, project browsing history, and activity timestamps strictly on your own personal device (localStorage). No activity tracking records are ever appended or written to Firestore cloud databases.
Section 4 • Semantic AI Vector Indexing

Vector Search & Pinecone Semantic Indexing

To enable intuitive semantic search beyond basic keyword matching, RE-CAPS utilizes the Pinecone Vector Database and the Pinecone Inference API:

  • Vector Embedding Model: Research abstracts, methodology summaries, and academic metadata are transformed into 1024-dimensional dense vectors using the multilingual-e5-large embedding model.
  • Dedicated Indexes: Primary capstone catalog (recaps-capstone) and staff knowledge base (recaps-staff-copilot).
  • User Privacy Isolation: Only approved, publicly archivable capstone research texts are embedded. Personal user records, names, passwords, security questions, and reading histories are strictly excluded from vector indexing.
Section 5 • AI Research Assistant

Cascading AI Copilot & Model Privacy

The RE-CAPS AI Research Assistant leverages Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) to synthesize grounded answers directly from the university's research catalog.

5.1 Cascading Multi-Provider Failover: To ensure high service availability, queries execute through an automated fallback cascade:

  1. Primary Provider: Mistral AI (open-mistral-nemo)
  2. First Failover: Groq API (llama-3.3-70b-versatile)
  3. Second Failover: Google Gemini API (gemini-2.5-flash)
  4. Final Fallback: OpenRouter API (Ling-3.0-Flash)

5.2 Strict Citation Grounding: The AI assistant is constrained to provide references and APA citations from real CTU capstone manuscripts retrieved during semantic vector search.

Zero Model Training Guarantee & Academic Disclaimer:
• User prompts and research questions are processed in real-time inference and are NEVER used to train public commercial AI models.
• The AI assistant is an advisory discovery companion. It is not an author, and its summaries do not substitute for rigorous literature review, faculty mentorship, or formal thesis defense evaluation.
Section 6 • Infrastructure Partners

External Infrastructure & Asset Guard

RE-CAPS partners with industry-standard cloud providers under strict data privacy agreements:

  • Cloudinary (Encrypted Document Pipeline): High-resolution digital manuscripts, capstone PDFs, and cover thumbnails are stored securely with encrypted HTTPS delivery and RBAC download guards.
  • Resend (Transactional Dispatch): Account verification notifications, password reset links, and institutional alerts are transmitted through encrypted SMTP/API channels without selling recipient information.
  • Render & Node.js Hosting: Back-end REST APIs operate on managed server containers with CORS domain isolation, connection pooling, and request rate-limiting.
Section 7 • Intellectual Property

Intellectual Property Rights & Academic Honesty

7.1 Ownership of Student & Faculty Works: In compliance with Republic Act No. 8293 (Intellectual Property Code of the Philippines), student researchers and faculty authors retain original copyright and moral rights over their submitted capstones and theses.

7.2 Institutional Archival License: By submitting research to RE-CAPS, authors grant Cebu Technological University - Daanbantayan Campus a non-exclusive, perpetual, royalty-free institutional license to archive, index, generate vector embeddings, and provide read-only educational access to the university community.

7.3 Zero Tolerance for Plagiarism: All work uploaded or referenced must adhere to ethical academic standards:

  • Direct copying, unattributed paraphrasing, or falsifying citations constitutes academic dishonesty.
  • All citations must conform to standard APA (American Psychological Association) 7th Edition format.
  • Violations will be forwarded to the CTU Academic Grievance & Disciplinary Committee.
Section 8 • Privacy Law Compliance

Republic Act No. 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012)

We are committed to upholding your statutory rights as an academic data subject under Philippine law:

  • Right to be Informed: Transparent disclosure of data schemas, database locations, and processing purposes.
  • Right to Access: View your profile, verified role credentials, bookmarked projects, and local research activities at any time.
  • Right to Rectification: Request corrections to erroneous names, institutional identification numbers, or academic affiliations.
  • Right to Erasure / Account Termination: Request complete account deletion and credential scrubbing by contacting system administrators.
  • Right to Data Portability: Export your local activity history using the built-in JSON / CSV activity exporter.
Section 9 • Code of Conduct

Acceptable Use Policy & RBAC Boundaries

9.1 Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Users must respect system boundaries based on their verified tier:

  • Students: Public catalog search, semantic AI assistance, and project detail view.
  • Faculty / Advisers: Student research advising, progress tracking, and evaluation feedback.
  • Librarians: Physical cataloging (call numbers, accession numbers), OCR ingestion, and thesis review.
  • Administrators: System configurations, batch catalog sync, and user account governance.

9.2 Prohibited Actions: Users shall not:

  • Deploy automated scrapers, crawlers, or harvesting scripts against RE-CAPS endpoints.
  • Attempt to bypass authentication gates, RBAC middleware, or tamper with security tokens.
  • Share accounts, impersonate university officials, or misrepresent academic credentials.
  • Upload infected files, destructive scripts, or exploit vulnerabilities.
